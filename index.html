English

Effective date: 26 October 2026 · Applies to the Bloom Blocks game for Android (package com.barttsy.bloomblocks), published by Barttsy.

Summary

Bloom Blocks does not collect, transmit or share any personal data. The game works entirely on your device. It has no internet permission, so it cannot send data anywhere.

Information we collect

We do not collect any information. The game has no user accounts or sign-in. It does not read your contacts, photos, location, camera, microphone or files. It does not use advertising IDs or other device identifiers.

Information stored on your device

To keep your game, the app saves the following on your device only, in its private app storage:

  • game progress: unlocked levels, stars and best scores;
  • in-game coins, lives and items you have earned or unlocked, and your garden layout;
  • the optional name you type for your garden;
  • settings: sound, music, vibration, language and reduced motion.

The app also reads your device's language setting, to pick the game language, and the device clock, for in-game timers. Both are used on the device only.

This data never leaves your device. The app does not back it up to your Google account. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes all of it permanently.

How we use information

The data stored on your device is used only to run the game: to remember your progress and settings between sessions.

Sharing with third parties

We do not share data with anyone. The app does not contain advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or social-media software development kits (SDKs), and it does not use Google Play services.

If you download the game from Google Play, Google may process information about the installation, such as download statistics and crash reports from users who chose to share them with Google, under Google's Privacy Policy. We have no control over that processing.

Permissions

  • Vibration (android.permission.VIBRATE): used only for short haptic feedback during play. You can turn it off in the game's settings.

The app requests no other permissions.

Data security

Your game data is kept in the app's private storage on your device, which Android protects from other apps. Because nothing is sent over a network, no data is exposed in transit or stored on our servers. We do not have any servers for this game.

Children's privacy

Bloom Blocks does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. No data is collected from any player, whatever their age.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the game adds features that handle data, for example advertising, we will update this policy before that version is released. The effective date above will change, and the Google Play listing will link to the updated version.

Contact

Questions about this policy: b.arttsy.game@gmail.com
